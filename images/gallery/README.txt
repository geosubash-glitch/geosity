GEO'S SECRET PIN-UP GALLERY
===========================
1. Put your pictures (.jpg / .gif / .png) in this folder.
2. Open index.html, find "var GALLERY" and add a line per picture:
     { src: "images/gallery/beach1.jpg", caption: "Malibu, summer '99" },
3. Only use pictures you have the right to publish.

To open the gallery on the site: click the pin-up girl on the RIGHT
side of the header 3 times fast, then say you're 18+.
