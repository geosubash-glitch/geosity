GEO'S SECRET PIN-UP GALLERY
===========================
The gallery fills itself with free vintage pin-up / bathing-suit pictures
from Wikimedia Commons (public domain or free licenses, credited on the page).
Change what it looks for in index.html -> COMMONS_SEARCHES.

To add your OWN pictures too:
1. Put them (.jpg / .gif / .png) in this folder.
2. In index.html, find "var GALLERY" and add a line per picture:
     { src: "images/gallery/beach1.jpg", caption: "Malibu, summer '99", credit: "photo by me" },
3. Only use pictures you have the right to publish.

To open the gallery on the site: click the pin-up girl on the RIGHT
side of the header 3 times fast, then say you're 18+.
