Reel Collage

Turns a reel into one tall image of its best shots: sharp, full-body, eyes open. It all happens on your phone, and nothing gets uploaded.

Getting a reel out of Instagram
  1. On the reel, tap Share (the paper plane). If you see Download, tap it. The video lands in your gallery.
  2. No Download button? Screen-record the reel instead. iPhone: Control Centre → Screen Recording. Android: Quick Settings → Screen record.
  3. Come back here and tap Choose a video.

On Android you can add this page to your home screen (Chrome menu → Add to Home screen). After that, any video in your gallery can be shared straight to Collage.

The first run downloads about 10 MB of models, which are kept for next time.
